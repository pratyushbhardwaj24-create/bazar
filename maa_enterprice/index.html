    Questions people ask at the counter

    Which tiles are best for floors in a Bihar summer and monsoon?

    Vitrified tiles stay cool underfoot and don't soak up water. Use a matt or anti-skid finish for bathrooms, verandas, stairs and anywhere that gets wet. Keep glossy tiles for bedrooms and halls.

    Do you sell marble or only tiles?

    Both. We have natural white marble and large marble-look tiles. Marble-look tiles cost less and need no polishing. Natural marble suits temple rooms and staircases.

    How much extra should I buy?

    About 10% more than the room area, and a little more for diagonal laying or rooms with many corners. Keep a few spare tiles from the same batch for repairs later.
